## Popular Chord Progressions

All popular music makes use of a limited number of chord progressions which are constantly repeated.

Continued practice of the popular chord progression sequences in this bo   t  nt t t   t  o chords used in the accompaniment of all popular music.

This chord progression manual has a separate section for each popular key. Any song in a chosen key can be harmonized effectively with thechords shown. Occasionally, only part of a progression may be used. For example, out of the progression C - C7 - F - Fm - C, only the section C - C7 - F, or F - Fm - C. or C7 - F - Fm, etc. need be used.

All instrumentalists, trying to develop a technique in "ad lib' playing will find this manual exceptionally useful. It is possible to improvise modern jazz passages on every chord progression shown here.

Songwriters may use this manual for finding the correct chords needed for the harmonization of any popular style melody.
