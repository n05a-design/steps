PPR Techno, 130 BPM, 4/4, straight (four on the floor)
From Drum Programming in Every Genre (pauseplayrepeat.com)

One folder: no swing. Every note sits on a 16th step (120 ticks at 480 ticks per quarter note).

Drums are on MIDI channel 10, laid out for Live's 909 Core Kit (Core Library, Drum Racks/Drum Machines):
kick 36 (C1 in Live), rim 37 (C#1), clap 39 (D#1), closed hat 42 (F#1), open hat 46 (A#1, choked by
the closed hat: both pads are in choke group 1), crash 49 (C#2, the peak), ride 50 (D2). Live calls
middle C (note 60) C3.

The loop: the kick on every beat (steps 1, 5, 9, 13) with a soft ghost kick on step 16 of bar 2, the
clap on 5 and 13, an open hat on every offbeat (3, 7, 11, 15), closed hats on the 16ths between, the
ride on the offbeats of bar 2, and the rim every 3 steps (bar 1: 1, 4, 7, 10, 13, 16; bar 2: 3, 6, 9,
12, 15) against the kick every 4. The rim starts over every two bars.

Files: Drums - Main 2 bars (the core loop), Drums - Build 8 bars (fill in bar 8), Drums - Peak 8 bars
(the ride on every bar, crash on 1), Drums - Fill 1 bar, Drums (the whole 16 bars: the build, then the
peak).
