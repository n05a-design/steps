FAQ

  • What are the delivery times for my order?

    We offer standard shipping, which typically takes:

    • 3-8 working days within the EU and USA
    • 7-30 days for other international locations

    For faster delivery, we offer expedited shipping, which usually takes 2-4 days, depending on your location.

  • What materials are the products made of?

    The products are made of high-quality, plastic-coated paper with metallic knobs, transparent plastic, and neodymium magnets.

  • Are these products suitable for beginners?

    Yes! Both products are designed to be beginner-friendly while offering advanced insights for more experienced players.

  • What’s your return policy?

    If you’re not satisfied with your purchase, we offer hassle-free returns within 14 days, provided the product is returned in perfect condition.
    Items must be shipped back to our warehouse. To initiate a return or for additional information, please contact us at info@chordfiles.com.
    Our team is ready to assist.

     

  • Where is the product being made?

    We are based in Slovenia (EU), and our products are made here as well. While we work with local printing partners, we assemble the products in-house by hand to ensure the highest quality.

  • Can I use these tools with alternate tunings?

    Currently, the diagrams are designed for standard tuning only. We may explore other tunings in future versions based on customer feedback.

  • Will there be digital versions of these products?

    At this time, these tools are available as physical products only. However, we may consider digital options in the future based on demand.

  • Do I need to buy both products to get value from them?

    Each product is valuable on its own, but they complement each other perfectly. Purchasing the bundle gives you a comprehensive set of tools for mastering blues and scales while saving money.

  • Why Not an App?

    We believe that phones, while powerful tools, can be a major distraction. With constant notifications and apps competing for your attention, it can be hard to stay focused. For truly immersive practice and learning, it’s better to use a dedicated tool. Our products are designed to inspire and support your musical journey without the interruptions of your phone, allowing you to focus on what matters most—your music.

Still Have Questions?
