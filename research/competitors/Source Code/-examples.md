### Add **modal interchange**.

 

If you wish to add chords from the parallel minor key move down from the main scale degrees (**I**, **IV**, or **V**) from the section ‘Main chords’ to any of the chords below.

 

CM – **EbM** – **AbM** – **Fm**
 I – **bIII – bVI – iv**
