PPR UK Garage, 132 BPM, 4/4
From Drum Programming in Every Genre (pauseplayrepeat.com)

Two folders, same notes:
- Swung: the swing from the lesson (Swing MPC 3000 16ths 64) is written into the drums.
  Every even 16th (steps 2, 4, ... 16) is 35 ticks late at 480 ticks per beat, 33.1 ms at 132 BPM.
  Use these in any DAW. Do not put a groove on them; they already swing.
- Straight: the same notes on the grid. Use these in Live with the Swing MPC 3000 16ths 64 groove
  on the clip, as the lesson does.

Drums are on MIDI channel 10, laid out for Live's Garage Kit (Core Library):
kick 36 (C1 in Live), snare 38 (D1, also the fill build), closed hat 42 (F#1), crash 49 (C#2).
Live calls middle C (note 60) C3.

The 2-step kick: beat 1 and the "and" of 3 (step 11), never beat 3; bar 2 adds a kick on step 4.

Files: Drums - Main 2 bars (the core loop), Drums - Verse 8 bars (fill in bar 8), Drums - Hook 8 bars
(crash on 1), Drums - Fill 1 bar, Drums (the whole 16 bars: the verse, then the hook).

This build is drums only, so there are no bass, keys or melody files.
